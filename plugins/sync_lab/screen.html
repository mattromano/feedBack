Sync Lab

Measures how far your picture, sound, guitar and drums are apart on this computer, and sets the offsets so notes are judged exactly when you play them. Uses the normal player, so it works in split view too (one result per panel).

Calibrate

  1. Pick who is playing. The 64-second "Sync Calibration" song opens (one note per beat: open A string for guitar and bass, snare for drums).
  2. Listen (screen goes black): close your eyes and play on every click.
  3. Watch (no clicks): play exactly when the notes hit the line.
  4. Play: normal. Then the results appear: Apply all.
Drum pad sounds are muted during calibration (they arrive late, see below). Guitar needs Note Detection on; tune to E standard.
